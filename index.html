About

Practical training that
actually prepares you

CodeAcharya is a specialized technical training organization for engineering colleges across India. We work directly with institutions — HODs, placement cells, and faculty — to design and deliver training that bridges the gap between academic curriculum and industry expectations.

We're not a platform or a pre-recorded course factory. Every program is facilitated by a practitioner with real industry experience, adapted to your institution's academic calendar and student profile.

Whether it's a semester-long skill course, an intensive placement bootcamp, or a Faculty Development Program — we deliver structured, hands-on learning that produces measurable outcomes.

Soft Skills & Aptitude

The skills that get you hired

Technical skills get you to the interview. Soft skills and aptitude get you the offer. Most engineering colleges teach neither — and most students discover this only when they're sitting across a recruiter for the first time.

CodeAcharya's Soft Skills & Aptitude programs are designed specifically for engineering students — covering the communication, reasoning, problem-solving, and professional skills that top companies consistently test for during campus placements.

Delivered as standalone workshops, semester modules, or add-ons to technical training — these programs transform how students present themselves, think under pressure, and work in teams.

Why this matters for placements

Leading recruiters consistently report that communication skills, logical reasoning, and professional attitude are the top differentiators between candidates with identical technical scores.

How It Works

From first call to
training day

A simple, institution-friendly process with no long-winded procurement cycles.

01
Initial Inquiry
A HOD, principal, or placement coordinator reaches out via email or phone. A brief call to understand your college's needs, student batch size, and academic timeline.
02
Custom Proposal
A tailored course proposal with clear learning outcomes, session breakdown, and transparent pricing — delivered within 48 hours of the first call.
03
MoU Signing
A simple agreement outlining scope, schedule, deliverables, and payment terms. No complex procurement. No unnecessary bureaucracy.
04
Training Begins
Sessions begin as per the agreed academic calendar — on-campus or online. Live, interactive, and project-driven. Progress shared regularly with the institution.
05
Certification & Report
Industry certificates issued to students. A detailed outcome report submitted to the institution, documenting skill acquisition and project completions.
